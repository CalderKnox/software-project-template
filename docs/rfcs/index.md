## Lifecycle

`draft → review → accepted / rejected → implemented`

Once an RFC is accepted, move implementation detail into
[design/](../design/index.md) or [specs/](../specs/index.md) and record the
outcome in [adr/](../adr/index.md). Do not rewrite accepted RFCs.
